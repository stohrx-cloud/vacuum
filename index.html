    What we fix most
    If your vacuum...It's usually
    Smells like burning rubberA stretched or snapped belt, or a brush roll seized with hair
    Has lost suctionA clog in the hose or wand, a full or wrong bag, or a blocked filter
    Cuts out or won't startA worn switch, a damaged cord or a thermal cutoff after overheating
    Trips the breakerA failing motor or a short in the cord. Stop using it and bring it in
    Brush roll won't spinBelt, bearings or the brush motor on powered heads
    Hose crackles or leaks airA split hose or a worn cuff. Hoses for most brands are in stock
    Motor sounds roughWorn carbon brushes or bearings. Often worth rebuilding on good machines
    Cordless runs for minutesA tired battery pack. We test it before you buy a new one

    04 / Central vacuums

    Installed, replaced and serviced in Halton homes.

    A central system puts the motor in the basement or garage and carries the dust out of your living space. You carry a light hose from room to room.

    New installs

    • System sized to your square footage and floors
    • Piping run through walls, closets and joists
    • Inlets, kick-plate sweeps and garage inlets

    Replacement and service

    • Power unit swaps on existing piping
    • Motor rebuilds and filter service
    • Inlet valves, low-voltage wiring and hose repair
    • Hoses, wands and attachment kits in stock

    Already built? Most finished homes can be retrofitted through closets, the garage and the basement ceiling, with little or no drywall opened. We quote it on site.
